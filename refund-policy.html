Refund Policy

This policy explains how refunds are handled for applicable service charges and payments.

Last updated: February 3, 2026

Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be applicable only for certain service fees paid directly to Book Me Loan if an application is cancelled prior to lender processing.

Non-Refundable Items

Verification charges, third-party processing fees, and lender-imposed fees are not refundable.

Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are processed within 7-10 business days and credited to the original payment method.

How to Request a Refund

Send your request with payment details, application ID, and reason to support@bookmeloan.com.

Need Help?

Our support team is available Mon–Sat, 9:00 AM–6:00 PM.
